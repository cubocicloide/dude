1. Find the ALB address (may take a minute to provision):

   kubectl get ingress {{ include "app.fullname" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} \
     -o jsonpath='{.status.loadBalancer.ingress[0].hostname}'

   Then open http://<that-hostname>/  (API is served under /api).
